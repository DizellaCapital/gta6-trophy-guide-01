                ⚠ Missable Watch List

                Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy reported as possibly missable. After the mission Down to Earth, find Lucia at Jason’s house in the morning — she mentions meditating — follow her to the balcony, accept, and finish that meditation before leaving Chapter 1. Everything else can be done after the story in free roam.

                • No mission replay, no New Game Plus. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 slots + autosave).
                • Cheats disable trophies and saving. Keep them off on your trophy save.
                • 5 hidden trophies still unconfirmed (21 Nov 2026): In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin. They are flagged method unconfirmed below — do not trust a single-source method for them.
