          From PowerPyx’s 100% Completion Checklist & Guide [3], read 20 Nov 2026. Progress made during the story counts; the list itself only appears after the credits, under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Nothing on it is missable.

          ⚠ Missable Watch List

          Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy reported as possibly missable. After the mission Down to Earth, find Lucia at Jason’s house in the morning — she mentions meditating — follow her to the balcony, accept, and finish that meditation before leaving Chapter 1. Everything else can be done after the story in free roam.

          • No mission replay, no New Game Plus. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 slots + autosave).
          • Cheats disable trophies and saving. Keep them off on your trophy save.
          • 5 hidden trophies still unconfirmed: In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin. They are flagged method unconfirmed in the tracker — do not trust a single-source method for them.

            Guide-level disputes

            • Story mission count: 91 (KeenGamer) vs 93 (Insider Gaming, Push Square) vs 95 (VGC) vs 122 including optional missions (GTABay). Same story either way — the counts differ on what they include.
            • Platinum hours: ~200 h (PowerPyx, KeenGamer, GTABase, OutputLag) vs “up to 300 hours or more” (Push Square, marked work in progress).
            • Meditations for 100%: 15 of 18 spots count toward the 100% checklist (PowerPyx 100% guide); the Positive Mental Attitude trophy needs all 18 (Beebom, GameTrek).
